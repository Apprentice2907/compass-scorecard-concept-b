Report content: the 81 pillar blocks

Each guest's report picks one block per area (3 of the 81), based on their three answers in that area. Every sentence is written in advance and approved once, so the report needs no AI when it runs.

Who each block speaks to

For youThe leader reading the report: recognition, a programme they can present to their board, parents and inspectors.
For your institutionThe business case: reputation, families choosing and staying, fewer complaints, lower risk and waste.
For students and familiesSatisfaction: food they enjoy and trust, answers they can see, a voice in what changes.

Where the blocks sit in the 5-page report

PageContentFrom the blocks
1 · Your scoreScore out of 45, stage, the three area cards, strongest area, cafeteria todayHeadline + band for each area
2 · Your three areasA half page per area: where you are today and your next stepToday + next step
3 · Each answer in depthWhat each of the 9 answers tells usExisting answer texts (quiz data)
4 · Your game planSteps for the next 30 days, this term and this year, with the points each addsNext steps, ordered by points gained
5 · What changesFor you, for your institution, for students and families; then next step with ChartwellsThe three audience lines from each area

How to read a block

The ID is the area and the points for its three answers, in question order. For example nutrition:3-1-5 means 3 points for menu planning, 1 for nutrition proof and 5 for ingredient standards (11/15, Building well). Bands: 13–15 Strong, 9–11 Building well, 3–7 Room to grow.

Copy rules applied (checked automatically)

  • No em dashes or exclamation marks; never "vendor", "canteen" or "food literacy".
  • No health or academic outcome promises; no statistics inside the blocks (sourced stats live on fixed pages).
  • Positive consultant tone: what works first, then the next step.
  • Block length: 56 to 137 words, average 85.
